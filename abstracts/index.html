Preparing your abstract

  • Use one A4 page with 25 mm margins and the template’s font sizes. The abstract text must not exceed 300 words.
  • Replace every bracketed placeholder. Use superscript numbers for affiliations and an asterisk for the presenting author.
  • Delete all instructional text and unused optional sections. Keep any figures, captions and references within the one-page limit.
  • Export a PDF and submit it with the editable Word or LaTeX source through the abstract submission form. Include any LaTeX figure files in a ZIP if needed.

LaTeX users: compile with pdfLaTeX. After downloading, return to the submission form to upload your completed abstract.
