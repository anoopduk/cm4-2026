Payment workflow

Before you pay

  1. Keep the form open. Do not submit or close the registration form yet.
  2. Pay the matching amount. Check the category carefully, complete payment, and retain the Razorpay confirmation.
  3. Return and submit. Enter the transaction details in the same registration form, then submit it.
